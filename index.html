Nine nights

Three nights each for Durga, Lakshmi and Saraswathi. The sundal list is one common rotation; every family has its own.

Vijayadashami

Tue 20 Oct

The tenth day. Children begin learning (vidyarambham), and one doll on each step is laid down to rest, so the golu can be packed away until next year.

Temples to visit in Tamil Nadu

After the golu, a yatra. Nine of the state’s best-loved temples, and a few places worth the journey.

A north-to-south route

One way to string all nine together. Tap a stop to see its temple.

    Most big temples close for a few hours in the afternoon and expect modest, traditional dress. Festival dates follow the Tamil calendar, so check timings with the temple before you travel.

    Beyond the temples

    Hills, sea and a little shopping for next year’s golu.

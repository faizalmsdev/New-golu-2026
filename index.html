Kalai arangam
Tap the golu to throw flowers. Drag the aarti plate clockwise; the bell rings each round.

The bommai box

Pick a doll, then tap a step to place it. Tap a doll on the steps to move it.

TopDeities, with the marapachi couple keeping watch
MiddleTemples, saints, weddings and kings
LowerTownsfolk: the Chettiar shop, vendors, families
BottomAnimals and plants. The steps climb from the earthly to the divine.

Nine nights

Three nights each for Durga, Lakshmi and Saraswathi. The sundal list is one common rotation; every family has its own.

Vijayadashami

Tue 20 Oct

The tenth day. Children begin learning (vidyarambham), and one doll on each step is laid down to rest, so the golu can be packed away until next year.

Thamboolam

No guest leaves a golu empty-handed. Before you go, please take thamboolam from us.

Betel leaves, areca nut, turmeric and kumkum, a coconut, and the sundal of the day, with all our love.
